01 · Opportunity
Investment Overview
Why Now

As AI makes goods and services cheaper, spending moves to what can't be automated: being there.

New money, seeking meaning

Austin startups raised a record $7.19B in 2025, led by AI. The people building the post-AI economy live here, and they are buyers of experiences, not things.

Spending shifts to wellbeing

The global wellness economy grew 7.9% in 2024, and wellness travel is outpacing travel overall. Gatherings that combine the two are where it lands.

The spring slot just opened

Envision, Costa Rica's late-February transformational festival, returned in 2026 and is now postponed indefinitely. Its audience needs a new spring gathering.

A rare sky that weekend

The spring equinox falls on Saturday, March 20, and the full moon peaks before sunrise on Monday, March 22. Both land inside the three days of Coherence.

Sources: Crunchbase · Global Wellness Institute · Envision · TheSkyLive

Investment Overview
Defensibility

Why this holds.

  • Zero travel days: most gatherings like this are hours from an airport; Coherence is 5 minutes from one and 15 from downtown
  • Two worlds in one room: consciousness and technology, in the city where both are booming
  • A community already gathered: UNIFY's global network of 2.8M+ across 70+ countries, plus Daybreaker and Bass Church audiences
  • A proven festival site: Carson Creek Ranch, where IMXP's founder ran Euphoria, with the amphitheater, camping and parking already in place
  • More than tickets: about a third of net revenue comes from partnerships, the RV Village, add-ons and vendors
